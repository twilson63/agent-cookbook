# Recipe: Give Your Claw an Email Address

**Platform:** Hyperclaw
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Time to set up:** 5 minutes

## The problem

Most AI assistants can't send or receive email on your behalf. They can draft text, but they can't actually touch your inbox — and connecting your real Gmail carries real risk.

Hyperclaw solves this by provisioning a dedicated AI inbox through [AgentMail](https://agentmail.to). Your Claw gets its own email address. It can send messages, read replies, and act on incoming mail — all from an address that's clearly linked to your agent, not your personal account.

## The agent's job

<Steps>

### Enable email on your Claw

Open your Claw in the Hyperclaw dashboard and click the **Email** tab. Click **Enable Email**. Hyperclaw calls the AgentMail API in the background and provisions a dedicated inbox for your Claw.

Your Claw receives two environment variables automatically:
- `AGENTMAIL_API_KEY` — authenticates all API calls
- `AGENTMAIL_INBOX_ID` — identifies your Claw's inbox
- `AGENTMAIL_EMAIL_ADDRESS` — the inbox's email address

### Confirm the inbox is live

Ask your Claw:

> What's my email address? Check your inbox and tell me if there are any messages.

It will read `$AGENTMAIL_EMAIL_ADDRESS` from its environment and call the AgentMail API to list messages.

### Send your first email

Ask your Claw to send a test message:

> Send a test email to me at [your personal email]. Subject: "Hello from my Claw". Keep it short.

Your Claw composes and sends the email via the AgentMail API. You should receive it within seconds.

### Reply handling

When someone replies to an email your Claw sent, the reply lands in the AgentMail inbox. Your Claw can check for new mail on demand or on a schedule:

> Check my inbox every morning and summarize any replies I've received overnight.

### (Optional) Set up real-time notifications

For instant reply handling, ask your Claw to configure a webhook so incoming emails trigger an automatic response:

> Set up a webhook so you're notified the moment a new email arrives in your inbox.

</Steps>

## What you need

- A Hyperclaw account with an active Claw
- Email enabled on the Claw (one click in the dashboard — no external API key required)

<Callout type="info">
Hyperclaw manages the AgentMail API key for you. You don't need to create an AgentMail account or configure anything outside the dashboard.
</Callout>

## What your Claw can do with email

| Task | How to ask |
|---|---|
| Send an email | "Email [person] about [topic]" |
| Check the inbox | "Any new emails?" |
| Reply to a thread | "Reply to the last email from Sarah and let her know I'll follow up Friday" |
| Summarize overnight mail | "Summarize everything in my inbox from the last 12 hours" |
| Forward a message | "Forward the invoice from Acme to accounting@company.com" |
| Draft and hold for review | "Draft a reply but don't send it yet — show me first" |

## Getting started

Open your Claw in the Hyperclaw dashboard, click the **Email** tab, and hit **Enable Email**. That's it.

<Callout type="tip">
Your Claw's email address is persistent — it stays the same across pod restarts and updates. Share it with people who should be able to reach your agent directly.
</Callout>
