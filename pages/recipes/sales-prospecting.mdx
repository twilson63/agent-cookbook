# Recipe: Sales Prospecting

**Department:** Sales / Business Development
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Time to set up:** 30–60 minutes

<ConceptVideo id="recipe-sales" title="Automating sales research and outreach" />

## Overview

The Sales Prospecting agent handles the research and outreach preparation work that currently consumes 2+ hours of a sales rep's morning: finding qualified prospects, enriching CRM records, surfacing recent news and signals, and drafting personalized first-touch emails. Reps receive a ready-to-send daily brief instead of spending their peak energy hours on manual research.

## Use Case

**Who this is for:** Account executives, SDRs, BDRs, and sales managers at B2B companies with a defined ICP (ideal customer profile).

**The problem it solves:** The average sales rep spends more than two hours every morning on prospecting prep — searching LinkedIn, reading company news, checking for trigger events, and trying to write emails that do not sound like templates. By the time they are done researching, their energy and focus are depleted for actual selling.

**Trigger:** A daily scheduled run, a new lead entering the CRM, or a rep manually kicking off research on a specific account.

## Agent Architecture

| Component | Purpose |
|---|---|
| CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive) | Source and destination for prospect data |
| Web search tool | Pulls recent news, job postings, LinkedIn activity |
| Email platform (Gmail, Outlook) | Sends outreach and logs replies |
| AI agent (Claude) | Synthesizes research and drafts personalized outreach |
| Sequencing tool (optional) | Automates follow-up scheduling (Apollo, Outreach, Salesloft) |

## Implementation Steps

<Steps>

### Define the target profile
You configure the agent once with your ideal customer profile. Be specific — vague ICPs produce poor results.

Good ICP definition:
> SaaS companies, 50–500 employees, Series B or later, VP of Finance or CFO as primary contact, US-based, using NetSuite or QuickBooks, and showing hiring signals for finance roles.

The agent uses this definition to filter, score, and prioritize prospects.

### Find qualified prospects
The agent searches across:
- LinkedIn (company pages, job postings, recent activity)
- News sources (funding announcements, expansions, leadership changes)
- Job boards (open roles signal priorities and budget availability)
- Intent data platforms if available (G2, Bombora, TechTarget)

It returns a scored list of prospects with confidence ratings and the specific signals that matched your ICP.

### Enrich the CRM
For each qualified prospect, the agent updates your CRM with current data:
- Accurate contact details (name, title, email, phone)
- Company firmographics (size, revenue estimate, tech stack)
- Recent activity signals (funding, hiring, expansions, leadership changes)
- Social proof touchpoints (mutual connections, shared groups, relevant content)

No manual data entry. The CRM stays current automatically.

### Research each prospect deeply
For prospects selected for outreach, the agent runs a deeper research pass:
- Recent company news (last 30 days)
- The prospect's recent LinkedIn posts or public activity
- Open job postings at the company (signals priorities and pain points)
- Competitor relationships or technology stack signals
- Relevant industry trends affecting their business

This context becomes the raw material for personalization.

### Draft personalized outreach
Using the full research context, the agent writes a first-touch email that references something specific to this prospect — not a merge-field template.

Example output:
> *Subject: Scaling the finance team at [Company] — a thought*
>
> *Hi Sarah,*
>
> *I noticed [Company] just posted three FP&A roles and recently moved from seed to Series B. That transition usually means the finance team is suddenly responsible for a lot more reporting complexity than the current stack was built for.*
>
> *We help finance teams at this exact stage — usually 3–8 person orgs — consolidate their close process from 12 days to under 5, without ripping out NetSuite.*
>
> *Worth 20 minutes to see if it fits your situation?*

### Sequence follow-ups
Based on email opens, link clicks, and replies, the agent schedules and sends follow-up messages at optimal intervals. No manual tracking or calendar reminders needed.

A typical sequence: Day 1 (initial email) → Day 4 (value-add follow-up with a relevant resource) → Day 9 (brief bump) → Day 16 (breakup email).

### Log everything to the CRM
Every email sent, every reply received, every link clicked — logged to the CRM contact record automatically. Reps open the CRM and see a full interaction history without typing a single note.

</Steps>

Email requirements:
- Subject line: specific, under 8 words, no clickbait

## Tips & Variations

**Trigger-based prospecting:** Instead of daily batch runs, configure the agent to fire on specific signals: a company announces a funding round, a key contact changes jobs, or a competitor loses a major customer. These "trigger events" are the highest-converting outreach moments.

**Champion tracking:** When a contact who previously used your product changes jobs, the agent detects the move and drafts a warm reengagement email immediately.

**Account-based selling:** For named accounts, run a weekly deep-research pass across all contacts at the account. Surface any new stakeholders, org changes, or buying signals to the account executive automatically.

**Objection preparation:** After the prospect replies with an objection, the agent drafts a response based on your objection-handling playbook and past successful replies in similar situations.

**Manager dashboard:** At the end of each week, the agent sends the sales manager a summary: prospects researched, emails sent, reply rates by segment, and which message variants are performing best.

## Getting Started

1. **Connect your CRM first** — this is the most important step. The agent needs read/write access to be useful. Most modern CRMs have native integrations or simple APIs.
2. **Write a precise ICP** — "mid-size tech companies" is not actionable. "SaaS companies, 50–500 employees, VP of Finance, recently raised Series B, US-based" is. Spend 30 minutes on this before running anything.
3. **Start with existing pipeline** — use the agent to enrich and research contacts you are already working with before turning it loose on net-new prospecting. This validates accuracy without risking your brand with new prospects.
4. **Review the first 20 email drafts personally** — spend time early correcting tone, specificity, and style. Most agents calibrate to your voice within 20–30 examples.
5. **Track reply rates by message variant** — the only metric that matters early is reply rate. Let data tell you which angles and formats work before scaling.
