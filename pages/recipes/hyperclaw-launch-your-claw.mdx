# Recipe: Launch Your First Claw

**Platform:** Hyperclaw
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Time to set up:** 10–15 minutes

## The problem

Running your own AI assistant used to mean setting up a server, managing Docker containers, and maintaining software updates. Most people want the assistant — not the infrastructure.

Hyperclaw handles all of that. You sign up, create a Claw, and in a few minutes you have a personal OpenClaw instance running in the cloud and connected to Telegram.

## What Hyperclaw does

Hyperclaw is a control plane that provisions and manages per-user OpenClaw pods. Each **Claw** is your own isolated AI assistant with its own identity, memory, skills, and connections. You control it through a browser dashboard or the Hyperclaw API. It runs continuously in the background — processing messages, running scheduled skills, and responding wherever you've connected it.

## The agent's job

<Steps>

### Create your account

Sign up at [claws.hyper.io](https://claws.hyper.io). You can use email/password or Google OAuth.

### Create a Claw

Click **New Claw** in the dashboard. Give it a name and choose a model provider. OpenRouter is the default — it gives your Claw access to dozens of models with a single API key.

Your Claw starts provisioning immediately. The dashboard shows the pod coming online.

### Connect to Telegram

Click the **Telegram** tab on your Claw's detail page. Follow the onboarding flow:

1. Create a new bot at [t.me/BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather) and copy the bot token
2. Paste the token into Hyperclaw
3. Hyperclaw links the bot to your Claw pod
4. Click the bot link to open a chat — your Claw is live

### Say hello

Send your Claw a message in Telegram. It responds using your chosen model. From here you can install skills, set up a schedule, and personalize your agent's identity.

### (Optional) Configure your Claw's identity

Your Claw reads a `SOUL.md` file in its workspace to understand who it is and how it should behave. You can edit this file from the **Files** tab or by asking your Claw directly:

> Read your SOUL.md. Now rewrite it to reflect that you are my personal research assistant focused on technology and business news.

</Steps>

## What you need

- A Hyperclaw account (free plan available at [claws.hyper.io](https://claws.hyper.io))
- A Telegram account
- An OpenRouter API key — or bring your own Anthropic, OpenAI, or Ollama key

## Expected results

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Account creation | 2 minutes |
| Claw provisioning | 1–3 minutes |
| Telegram connection | 5 minutes |
| First message | Under 10 seconds |

Within 15 minutes of signing up, you have a personal AI assistant running 24/7, reachable on your phone, and ready to install skills.

## Getting started

Head to [claws.hyper.io](https://claws.hyper.io) and create your account. If you run into issues during the Telegram connection step, the onboarding bot will walk you through it step by step.

<Callout type="tip">
Your Claw pod persists npm and pip installs across restarts under `/home/node/.openclaw`. Installed skills and scheduled tasks survive pod restarts automatically.
</Callout>
