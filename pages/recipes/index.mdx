## How to read a recipe

Each recipe follows the same structure:

1. *The problem* — what you're trying to solve and why it matters
2. *Steps* — exactly what to do, in order, with commands you can copy
3. *What to expect* — what success looks like when you're done
4. *Tips* — common mistakes to avoid and ways to extend the recipe

<Callout type="tip">
Start with [Launch Your First Claw](/recipes/hyperclaw-launch-your-claw) if you haven't created a Claw yet. The other recipes assume you already have one running.
</Callout>
