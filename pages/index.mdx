## What every Claw needs

The onboarding guide builds the four capabilities that form the foundation of any useful agent:

| Capability | What it gives your Claw |
|---|---|
| Identity | A persistent name, voice, and operating style |
| Output (ZenBin) | A way to publish and share work as public pages |
| Input (Web Search) | Access to current information beyond training data |
| Memory | Durable recall that survives session restarts |
| Scheduling | The ability to act without being asked |

The morning brief — Step 6 of onboarding — is the capstone that exercises all five in one workflow.
