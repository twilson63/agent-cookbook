## What is HyperClaw?

HyperClaw is an agent platform. Each Claw is your own dedicated AI pod — running OpenClaw, connected to your channels, and ready to take on work:

| What your Claw can do | How |
|---|---|
| Chat with you on Telegram | Connected during setup, always on |
| Receive and send email | AgentMail gives your Claw its own inbox |
| Run custom skills | Markdown skill files define repeatable behaviors |
| Remember things between sessions | Workspace files persist across restarts |
| Be managed programmatically | Full REST API for create, configure, and deploy |

Your Claw starts as a general assistant. The recipes and setup guides here show you how to make it specific — a developer agent, an email responder, an automation worker — whatever your workflow needs.
