# Onboarding Your HyperClaw Agent

This guide is written for two audiences at once: the human who just spun up a new Claw, and the Claw itself. Work through the steps together, or paste the bootstrap prompt below to let your Claw self-onboard.

## The bootstrap prompt

Copy this and send it to a fresh Claw as your first message:

```text
You are being onboarded as my HyperClaw agent. Your onboarding guide is at:

  https://cookbook.scoutos.live/onboarding

Read the Start Here page, then work through each step in order:

  1. Identity     — set up SOUL.md, USER.md, and IDENTITY.md
  2. ZenBin       — configure your publishing keypair
  3. Web Search   — verify you can search the web
  4. Memory       — establish your memory workflow
  5. Scheduling   — set up your first scheduled task
  6. Morning Brief — build and publish your first brief

Fetch each guide from the cookbook URL above before starting that step.
Complete each step fully and verify it works before moving to the next.
When you finish step 6, send me a summary of your setup and the URL of your first published brief.
```

That's it. Your Claw will fetch each guide, follow the steps, and report back when it's done.

---

## What every Claw needs

After onboarding, every Claw should have four things:

| Pillar | What it gives your Claw | Set up in |
|---|---|---|
| *Identity* | A name, voice, and operating style that persist | Step 1 |
| *Output* | A way to publish and share work (ZenBin) | Step 2 |
| *Input* | A way to search the web for current information | Step 3 |
| *Memory* | Durable recall that survives session restarts | Step 4 |
| *Autonomy* | The ability to run tasks on a schedule | Step 5 |

Step 6 — the Morning Brief — is the capstone. It exercises all five pillars in one workflow: your Claw searches the web, formats a brief, publishes it to ZenBin, and delivers it on a schedule. If the morning brief works, your Claw is fully operational.

## After onboarding

Once your Claw completes the six steps, it's ready to specialize. Head to [Specializations](/tools) to choose a path based on what you want your Claw to do.

<Callout type="tip">
Don't skip steps. Each one builds on the last. A Claw without memory can't maintain a good morning brief. A Claw without ZenBin can't publish one. Work through them in order.
</Callout>
