# Step 7 — Clawvisor

*For agents:* Connect each integration your human uses. Verify each connection with a live test before marking it done. Save connection status and any relevant IDs to TOOLS.md.

---

Clawvisor is the integration layer for your Claw — the bridge between your agent and the tools you actually work in every day. Without it, your Claw lives in a chat window. With it, your Claw can read your email, check your calendar, post to Slack, open GitHub issues, and take action across your entire stack.

This is what turns a capable assistant into a working teammate.

<Steps>

### Open Clawvisor

Access Clawvisor from the HyperClaw dashboard:

**Dashboard → Your Claw → Clawvisor**

You'll see a list of available integrations. Connect each one that applies to your workflow.

### Connect your email

Email is the highest-leverage connection for most people. Your Claw can read incoming messages, draft replies, send on your behalf, and use your inbox as a task queue.

```text
Connect my email to Clawvisor. I use [Gmail / Outlook / other].
Walk me through the OAuth flow.
```

Once connected, verify with a live read:

```text
What are the last 3 unread emails in my inbox?
```

If your Claw can describe them, email is working.

<Callout type="info">
Your Claw will only read and send email when you instruct it to, or when a skill explicitly includes email actions. It does not monitor your inbox passively unless you set up a scheduled email-review task.
</Callout>

### Connect your calendar

Calendar access lets your Claw include your schedule in the morning brief, avoid booking conflicts, and remind you about upcoming commitments.

```text
Connect my Google Calendar (or Outlook Calendar) to Clawvisor.
```

Verify with:

```text
What do I have scheduled for tomorrow?
```

A working calendar connection returns your actual events. Update your morning-brief skill to include today's calendar:

```text
Update my morning-brief skill to include my calendar events for the day as a new section called "On the calendar."
```

### Connect Slack

Slack integration lets your Claw post updates, monitor channels for mentions, and surface important threads without you having to dig through it yourself.

```text
Connect Slack to Clawvisor. I want my Claw to be able to post to channels and read messages.
```

Choose which workspace and which channels your Claw should have access to. Start narrow — your Claw doesn't need to see every channel on day one.

Verify with a test post:

```text
Post "Claw connected ✅" to the #general channel (or any low-traffic channel).
```

### Connect GitHub

GitHub integration is essential if you're using the Developer Agent specialization, but useful for anyone who tracks work via issues or PRs.

```text
Connect GitHub to Clawvisor. I want my Claw to be able to read repos, open issues, and create PRs.
```

Verify with:

```text
List the open pull requests in [your main repo].
```

<Callout type="warning">
Grant GitHub access at the repo level, not the org level, until you've verified your Claw behaves correctly. You can expand permissions later.
</Callout>

### Save connection status to TOOLS.md

Once connections are live, document them:

```bash
cat >> ~/TOOLS.md << 'EOF'

## Clawvisor Integrations

- Email: [Gmail / Outlook] — connected, verified
- Calendar: [Google / Outlook] — connected, verified
- Slack: [Workspace name] — connected, channels: [list]
- GitHub: [Org or repo] — connected, verified

## Notes
- GitHub access is repo-scoped, not org-scoped
- Slack: Claw can post to #general and #dev only
EOF
```

</Steps>

## What Clawvisor unlocks

Once connected, your Claw can coordinate across your full stack without switching context:

| What you say | What your Claw does |
|---|---|
| "Clear my morning" | Reads calendar, declines or reschedules low-priority meetings |
| "Catch me up on Slack" | Surfaces threads where you were mentioned since yesterday |
| "Handle the support emails" | Reads inbox, drafts replies, flags anything that needs you |
| "What's open on the repo?" | Lists PRs and issues, grouped by priority |
| "Send the brief to the team" | Publishes to ZenBin and posts the link in Slack |

Each of these combines two or more integrations — which is why Clawvisor is core setup, not optional.

## Add more integrations

Beyond the four core connections, Clawvisor supports additional services. Connect them as your workflows require:

- *Notion* — read and write to your workspace: meeting notes, wikis, project databases
- *Linear / Jira* — create issues, update statuses, and surface blocked work automatically
- *Zoom / Meet* — join calls, generate summaries, and create action items from transcripts
- *Stripe / QuickBooks* — monitor revenue, flag anomalies, and pull financial context into briefs
