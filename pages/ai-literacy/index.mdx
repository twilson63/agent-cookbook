# AI Literacy: From Prompts to Agent Teams

A three-module course that takes you from writing better prompts to running multi-agent workflows — with a short video and hands-on pod exercise for every lesson.

No coding required. Built for anyone who works with AI and wants to stop guessing and start getting results.

## How to use this course

Each lesson has three parts:

1. *Watch* — a 30-second video framing the idea
2. *Read* — the full essay going deep on the concept
3. *Practice* — a pod exercise using your own real work

Work through lessons in order the first time. The concepts build on each other — Module 1 is the foundation for everything in Modules 2 and 3.
